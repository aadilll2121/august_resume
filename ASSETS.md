# Assets and attribution

## Design reference

Requested reference: https://www.keivalya.com/

The new implementation uses Aadil's content with a centred profile, narrow content column, teal highlights, timeline cards, and light/dark control. The reference's biographical text, original project images, analytics, and personal links are not distributed.

## Typography

- Primary font: `Bookman Old Style`, resolved from the visitor's installed fonts.
- Bundled fallback: TeX Gyre Bonum Regular and Bold, distributed unmodified.
- Upstream: https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre
- Font files: https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/tex-gyre/opentype/texgyrebonum-regular.otf and https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/tex-gyre/opentype/texgyrebonum-bold.otf
- Family authors: Bogusław Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki; GUST e-foundry.
- Licences: `dist/assets/fonts/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt` and `dist/assets/fonts/LPPL-1.3c.txt`.
- The primary font and fallback are different font files. No proprietary Bookman Old Style font is embedded in this package.

## Project media

`Aadil_ETAP_Project_Report.pdf`, `Aadil_ETAP_Project_Poster.pdf`, and `Aadil_Project_Expo.jpg` were supplied by Aadil and remain unchanged. `ETAP_Poster_Preview.webp` and `ETAP_Distribution_Layout.webp` were rendered from the original poster. The header portrait is a CSS crop of the supplied photograph, without altering the file.

## Graphics

The motor-control thumbnail, AC-conversion thumbnail, and favicon are editable SVG artwork. They are conceptual illustrations. GitHub and LinkedIn symbols identify the linked social profiles. Company abbreviations are neutral labels and are not presented as official logos.
